La familia

Bienvenidos, familia.

This is our family history, put together from church books, civil registers, and death records found on FamilySearch, and from the stories our parents and grandparents have shared.

It follows the Rincón line back to Zacatecas in the 1800s, and the Jiménez and Muñoz families of Cañadas de Obregón, Jalisco. Every fact is labeled so you can see what a record proves and what is still a family story.

Know a name, a date, or a story we're missing? Tell Carlos Jr. and we'll add it. And there's a new branch on the way: Carlos Jr. and Diana's baby girl arrives in December 2026.

        What the labels mean

        • Verified An original record (or its official index) proves this person, their relationship, or this fact.
        • Likely From a shared family tree or a record that fits but isn’t fully proven.
        • Family story Told by the family and not yet confirmed by a record.

        A person’s badge in the tree is Verified only when a record proves who they are and how they’re related. People known mainly from the FamilySearch shared tree show Likely. A family-confirmed link marks a connection the family knows but no record shows yet. Open anyone to see the label on every fact.

        Researched with public records from FamilySearch. Place history from the linked Wikipedia articles; historic photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited under each one. Chapter scenes are illustrations, not real photos.
        Living family members are shown by name only, to keep them private.
